  • Correctly separates observed statistic from a permutation-based null.
  • Interpretation is present in comments (helpful), though a formal report would strengthen Criterion 10 evidence.

No penalty for stylistic differences — student implementation is algorithmically clear and aligns with the taught patterns.

D. Statistical & Computational Rigor

  • Observed correlation computed correctly on adjacent pairs.

E. Error Handling & Robustness

Comparison with Example Solution
